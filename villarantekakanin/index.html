Freshness and Storage

  • Kakanin is best eaten the same day it is made.
  • Refrigerate leftovers in a clean, airtight container at 4°C or below. They keep for about 2 to 3 days; items with cheese or dairy may keep for less.
  • Keep Tikoy sealed in a cool, dry place if shelf-stable, or refrigerate it if fresh or homemade. Slices can be pan-fried.
  • Refrigerate Cassava Flan promptly and eat it within 2 to 3 days.
  • Reheat by steaming for a few minutes or microwaving in short intervals while covered.
  • Do not leave perishable kakanin at room temperature for more than 2 hours, or 1 hour if above 32°C.
