Our Story

How Villarante's Kakanin came to be

Our Heritage

Villarante's Kakanin started as a small family business rooted in the rich culinary traditions of the Philippines. Our lola Villarante began making kakanin for family gatherings, and her delicious creations quickly became the talk of the neighborhood.

Today, we continue her legacy by preparing each item with the same love and attention to detail, using traditional methods passed down through generations.
